The playbook, minus the handoffs.

Most freelancers run four tools and a spreadsheet that glues them together. Here is the same month, two ways.

The usual month

  1. Copy the accepted proposal into an invoice by hand.
  2. Export hours, multiply by your rate, paste the total.
  3. Write the reminder email yourself, three times.
  4. Edit the invoice to add a late fee, and hope nobody asks what it said before.
  5. Enter the bill as an expense too, and count it twice at tax time.

With Ledgendary

  1. The option your client picks is the invoice.
  2. Logged hours land on it at the agreed rate.
  3. Reminders go out on your schedule.
  4. A late fee posts as its own entry. The invoice stays as agreed.
  5. A paid bill is a bill. Spend counts once.
