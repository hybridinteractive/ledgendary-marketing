Vendors, W-9s and bills, without the filing cabinet.

Track each vendor's W-9 from request to on file. Keep the kind of tax id and its last four digits, never the full number. And when you pay a bill, it's a bill: not an expense as well.

  • A full tax id is never stored. There isn't even a place for one.

    Receipt: Kestrel Photo's tax id field.

    True story. Kestrel's record says EIN, ending 4821. That's enough to recognise the form Sam already holds, and nothing a breach could use.

  • W-9 collection is a lifecycle, not a checkbox.

    Receipt: Kestrel's W-9 history.

    Requested Oct 2, received Oct 9, on file Oct 10. "We asked and they haven't sent it" is a state you can see.

  • Paying a bill never doubles as an expense.

    Receipt: BILL-0017's payment.

    $2,800.00 paid to Kestrel on Nov 14. It shows once, under bills, and November's expenses don't include it.
