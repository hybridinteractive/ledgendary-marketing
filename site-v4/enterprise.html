S01E03 · The Outside Signature

An organisation's unanchored entries are reduced to one Merkle root, and a third-party timestamp authority signs it. The evidence sits in storage nobody can delete before its retention date, us included. Someone with full database access could rewrite a chain. They can't make the timestamp authority have signed a different root last March.

True story. Signed by someone who isn't us.
