Ledgendary mark

The LE mark is three horizontal bars. Their rounded left ends line up into the L, the long gold bottom bar is its foot, and the two gaps make the E. The tile is the app's dark-mode frame (#0b0b0d) and the accent is gold (#ffc629). The mark and the wordmark can each be used on their own.

Construction

One number builds the whole mark: the module t, the bar thickness, which is one eighth of the tile. Every edge sits on a 16-unit grid, so the same drawing lands on whole pixels at every size that's a multiple of 16.

24 px isn't a multiple of 16, so it has its own drawing with the same bar proportions, slightly larger in the tile. That's the usual optical correction for small icons.

Three beats

The three bars are the three beats of the line the brand is named after. Nobody has to know, but it explains why the foot is gold and why it always arrives last.

Sizes

The mark at its standard sizes on light and dark. Every size uses whole pixels.

Motion

The mark moves by drawing its bars, never by scaling them, so the round ends stay round in every frame. A bar at length zero is a dot, so before a reveal the mark is three stacked dots, an ellipsis, and the gold dot waits before it lands. Timing uses one unit, the beat (160 ms). Every duration and delay is a whole number of beats, just as every measurement is a whole number of modules.

Use

Inline the mark and link motion/motion.css, or use the animated SVG files as-is. With reduced motion turned on, every version shows the still mark.

Kit

Every file is self-contained SVG with outlined text, so it has no fonts or external references. Click a tile to open its file.

Wordmark and tagline

These work on their own, with no mark beside them. The wordmark is Mona Sans 800 at 116% width and −3% tracking, outlined. The tagline is Mona Sans 520: "From proposal to … wait for it … paid." The ellipses stand apart from the words. On dark they are gold, which measures 11.4 to 12.5 to 1. On light they take the tagline's own colour, because gold on white measures 1.5 to 1 and fails at any size.

Files. The mark kit is in logo/mark/, the animated marks and motion.css are in logo/motion/, and the wordmark and tagline are in logo/wordmark/. logo/build/build.py regenerates all of them. motion.css is the source for the animated files.
