Ledgendary mark

Five LE marks made of three horizontal bars and nothing else. The flush left edge is the L, the long gold bottom bar is its foot, and the two gaps make the E. Bar length decides which letter reads first. The colours are unchanged: the tile is the app's dark-mode frame (#0b0b0d) and the accent is gold (#ffc629). Each option is a full SVG kit with logomark, lettermark, gold container, negative space and lockups. The wordmark and tagline are separate files, so the mark and the wordmark can each be used on their own. Click any tile to open the SVG.

Files. Each option is in its own folder (logo/a/, logo/b/ and so on), and the shared wordmark and tagline are in logo/wordmark/. Every SVG is self-contained: the text is outlined, so there are no fonts or external references. logo/build/build.py regenerates the whole kit.

Construction. Marks use a 32-unit grid with 4-unit bars on even coordinates, so the 16px favicon renders on whole pixels. logomark-on-dark adds a 1px hairline (white at 10%) so the tile doesn't sink into dark panels. Negative space is one colour with the letters cut out, for stamps, embossing and single-ink print.

Wordmark. Mona Sans 800, width 116%, tracking -3%, outlined. Tagline: Mona Sans 520, "From proposal to… wait for it… paid." The wait sits right before the money lands, and the line still says what the product does without the wink.
