Ledgendary mark

The final LE mark: three horizontal bars. The rounded left ends line up into the L, the long gold bottom bar is its foot, and the two gaps make the E. The tile is the app's dark-mode frame (#0b0b0d) and the accent is gold (#ffc629). The mark and the wordmark can each be used on their own. Click any tile to open the SVG.

Files. The mark kit is in logo/mark/, and the wordmark and tagline are in logo/wordmark/. Every SVG is self-contained: the text is outlined, so there are no fonts or external references. logo/build/build.py regenerates the whole kit.

Construction. The mark uses a 32-unit tile with one module, the bar thickness of 5: gaps of 2.5, bars of 15, 10 and 20, round ends, and a 6-unit inset on every side. The favicon keeps those proportions with a module of 4, so every edge lands on a whole pixel at 16 and 32 px. logomark-on-dark adds a 1px hairline (white at 10%) so the tile doesn't sink into dark panels. Negative space is one colour with the letters cut out, for stamps, embossing and single-ink print.

Wordmark. Mona Sans 800, width 116%, tracking -3%, outlined. Tagline: Mona Sans 520, "From proposal to… wait for it… paid." The wait sits right before the money lands, and the line still says what the product does without the wink.
