The playbook.

Everything else an agency runs on, in the same product and on the same books. Search it the way you would in the app.

K Demo data
  • EstimatesProposalsPrice a job without options. Convert an accepted estimate into an invoice at the rate it was agreed at, even if your default rate changes later.
  • Recurring invoicesInvoicesRetainers bill themselves on a schedule. One invoice per period, never two, even if the scheduler runs twice.
  • Reminders and late feesInvoicesPolite nudges before and after the due date. A late fee posts its own entry and raises the total in the same step, so the invoice and the books never disagree.
  • Credit notesInvoicesGive money back as credit a client can apply to the next invoice. Every cent of issued credit is either still available or applied somewhere you can see.
  • Client portalPortalYour client chooses an option, signs, reads invoices and pays, all from the link you send.
  • Card and bank paymentsPaymentsClients pay online by card or bank. A payment posts to the books as it lands, and a provider retry never records it twice.
  • Expenses and receiptsExpensesLog spend with the receipt attached. Fix an amount later and the books restate the period it belongs to, instead of quietly rewriting it.
  • Bills and vendorsBillsTrack what you owe and when it is due. Paying a bill never counts twice as an expense, and a full tax id is never stored.
  • Bank feedsBankingBring in bank transactions and match them to what you already recorded, so the bank and the books tell the same story.
  • Multi-currencyInvoicesBill in your client's currency. Each document keeps the exchange rate it was issued at, and reports read your base currency.
  • ReportsReportsProfit and loss, balance sheet, aging, tax and more, all read from the same posted books and all as of any date you pick.
  • Period closeBooksClose a month and nothing dated in it can change. Closing and reopening ask for your password again.
  • Roles and approvalsSettingsOwners, administrators, members and a read-only accountant. Members see the projects they are on; an owner or administrator approves each submitted week before it bills.
  • Nothing in the playbook matches that. Try "invoice" or "time".
