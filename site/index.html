  1. Propose

    Send one proposal with three ways to say yes. Essentials, the full build, or the build with a retainer. Each option carries its own scope, fixed fee and time estimate, and nobody retypes them later.

  2. Decide

    Dana opens the client portal, compares the options side by side, picks B and signs with her name. The record keeps who signed and when.

  3. Track

    The team logs hours against the job in a weekly grid. A meeting from Maya's calendar shows up as a private draft that only she can see, until she accepts it.

  4. Bill

    The fixed fee Dana signed for becomes the first invoice line. The 80 hours the team logged become the second, at the rate she agreed. The work came in on the estimate, so the invoice is the $42,000 she was quoted, and every line says where it came from.

  5. Get paid

    Reminders go out on a schedule you set. Dana pays by card from the portal. If an invoice runs late, the late fee posts as its own entry; the invoice itself is never rewritten.
