  1. Propose

    Send Dana three ways to say yes. She picks B in your portal and signs with her name. The option, its fee and its estimate come along from here; nobody retypes them.

  2. Track

    Log hours in a weekly grid. A meeting on your calendar arrives as a private draft only you can see, with its title and duration and nothing else, until you accept it.

  3. Bill

    The fixed fee becomes line 1. The 20 hours you logged for Northwind become line 2, at the $150 Dana agreed. $9,500, exactly the estimate, and every line says where it came from.

  4. Get paid

    Reminders wait on a schedule you set once. Dana pays by card in the portal before any of them is needed. Had she paid late, the fee would post as its own entry.

  5. Posted

    The invoice and the payment post themselves, double-entry, the moment they happen. Each entry carries the fingerprint of the one before it.
