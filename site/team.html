  1. Log

    Theo logs his week in a grid. While it's a draft it's his to edit, and his private calendar drafts stay his alone.

  2. Submit

    Friday, Theo submits. The week now waits for an owner or administrator, and nobody edits it in the meantime.

  3. Approve

    Monday morning Sam approves it, and an approved week locks. Two approved weeks add up to the 80 hours ready to bill.

  4. Invoice

    The approved hours land on INV-0042 at the $150 Northwind agreed, next to the fixed fee from option B. $42,000, and nobody typed an hour.

  5. A note, nothing else

    Once an entry is on an invoice, its hours are what the client was billed. Theo can add a note to Tuesday. He can't change it.
