The playbook: no gaps, no doubles, no peeking.

The questions an auditor asks first, answered by how the books are built rather than by a policy somebody has to follow.

MV Meridian & Vale Three checks Demo data

  1. No gaps

    Every document number comes from one counter, inside the transaction that creates the document. A save that fails takes its number back with it.

    Last issuedINV-08812

  2. No doubles

    A payment the provider sends twice is applied once. A refund is charged at the provider first and recorded second, so the books never show money back that never left.
