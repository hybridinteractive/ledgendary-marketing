# Home (marketing)

Scope: the Ledgendary marketing home page, `site/index.html`. Mode: Persuade. Static HTML, CSS, JS.
Audience: agency owners and operators, from one person to several hundred. Action: join the waitlist (invite-only alpha).
Proof: the product itself, shown working as live UI with labelled demo data. No customers, logos, testimonials or metrics exist; none invented. No prices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The product is the hero. One job (Northwind Co, "Site build", option B at $42,000) travels the whole page, proposal to paid to posted books, with the same figures on every screen, which is the proof that it is one ledger. Refuses the category default: split hero with a floating dashboard screenshot over a row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Daylight SaaS. White and cool mist (#ffffff, #f4f5f7) ground, ink #0d0e12, one committed accent "legendary gold" #ffc629 used only as fill (highlighter behind words, primary button with ink text, the Paid moment, chain links). App semantics inside UI only: paid green, overdue red, selection blue. One dark ink chapter for the books. Mona Sans variable: display wide (wdth 112 to 125, weight 800, tight), text at wdth 100, tabular figures everywhere money appears. Real UI chrome: 1px hairlines, 10 to 14px radii, soft offset shadows, Lucide icons.

STORY: Visitor sees a proposal get picked, invoiced and paid within seconds; understands proposals, time, invoicing, portal and books are one product; scrolls the job's story; meets the books and their tamper-evidence; sees it scales from solo to large agency; joins the waitlist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav (wordmark, Product, Agencies, The books, Join the waitlist). Left-aligned wide display headline across ~70% width, subline, inline waitlist field plus gold button. The live product window starts at ~58% of the viewport height, full content width, top half visible, already playing: option B chosen, invoice sent, "wait for it…", Paid. Pause control and scene chips on the window.

FORM: Category standard at full craft (canon exit taken by Michael after rejecting the dealt hand), bar set by Linear, Stripe and Mercury. Signature interaction: the hero job loop and the sticky scroll chapter where one product window morphs between proposal, portal, week grid, invoice, payment and journal. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
