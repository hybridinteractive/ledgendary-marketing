# Look v5: Prove it (audience landing pages)

Scope: the v5 look's three audience pages, `site-v5/solo.html`, `site-v5/team.html`, `site-v5/enterprise.html`, plus a small `site-v5/index.html` that links them. Mode: Persuade. Content: `.impeccable/briefs/audiences.md`. Truth: `PRODUCT.md`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every claim has a receipt. Each feature is stated in one plain, confident sentence, and next to it a "Prove it" control opens the exact piece of product that proves it: a loupe zooms into the UI detail, the evidence row, the refused edit, the signed timestamp. "True story." is the page's refrain. Refuses the category default and all three earlier looks.

OWN-WORLD: Cool daylight. Ground #eceef2 with white #ffffff working surfaces; graphite ink #15161a; secondary text tinted cool (#565b68). One accent, electric violet #6b2cff, as fill (white text, about 7:1) for actions, the loupe ring, the "proven" stamp and Paid. Product UI drawn crisp and dense in light theme with 1px lines #d9dce3. Depth from layering and a single soft shadow recipe; the loupe is the one lifted object. Type: Geologica (variable weight and slant); display 700 to 800 upright, tight; claims set large at 500; a slight slant used only for the "True story." stamp. Tabular figures in columns.

STORY: The visitor reads a claim, doesn't believe it, presses Prove it, and the product shows it. By the end the visitor has opened every receipt and the waitlist is the obvious next step.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Light ground. Nav. A two-line headline, subline, waitlist form. Beside or under it, the audience's boldest claim as a single line with its Prove it control already half-open: the loupe hovering over the product evidence (solo: the late fee posted as its own entry; team: the private draft only Maya sees; enterprise: the timestamp authority's signature on last March's root).

FORM: Category standard at full craft, fifth look; bar Linear, Stripe, Mercury, Apple's feature pages (macro product close-ups). Signature interactions: (1) Prove it: a claim row expands, the product panel slides in and a circular loupe magnifies the exact detail, keyboard operable, with the evidence also stated as text; (2) macro-to-full: on scroll, a product close-up zooms out to the whole app screen it came from. seed: none (canon exit, new-work.md §3 "The standing exit"; user-pinned alternate, no roll). Michael, 2026-11-18: "reuse today's 3 looks, and add another 2 different looks".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
