## Direction contract

THESIS: You drive the demo. Instead of watching a loop, the visitor plays the client (picks option B, signs) and then the studio (sends, waits for it, gets paid), and the page answers like the real app. Refuses the category default and refuses v1's daylight-autoplay arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched. A saturated ultramarine field #2433ff owns the hero, the books chapter and the final call; white #ffffff sections between; ink #0b0c1c text on white; white text on blue. One accent, acid lime #c8ff3d, used only as fill (Paid, the chosen option, highlights, primary button with ink text); the one exception is the keyboard focus ring on blue grounds, a lime outline (about 6:1 on #2433ff), amended 2026-11-18 after the finish review. Product windows are white, crisp, with soft blue-tinted offset shadows on the field. Type: Funnel Display (heavy, 700 to 800, tight) for display, Funnel Sans for text and UI, tabular figures for money.

STORY: Visitor gets the deal done themselves within the first screen, then sees one figure ($42,000) hold still while the screens around it change, adds time and watches the invoice change, meets the books, scales the team from one to three hundred, joins the waitlist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Blue field edge to edge. Nav in white. Headline big and white across the top-left ("Close the deal. Get paid. Keep the books." or stronger), short subline, waitlist field plus lime button on the field. Right of or under the headline, the interactive client-portal card at large scale with a prompt chip "You're the client. Pick one." Three options visible; clicking one starts the play.

FORM: Category standard at full craft, second alternate; bar Linear, Stripe, Mercury, Ramp. Signature interactions: (1) play-it-yourself hero (client, then studio; idle 8s runs it automatically; reduced motion shows the final state); (2) "one number, five screens": $42,000 pinned in the centre on scroll while proposal, portal, invoice, payment and journal slide in around it. seed: none (canon exit; alternate of a canon direction, no roll). Michael, 2026-11-18: "try another one, different style, same winks, same animated stuff, just as good in marketing"

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
