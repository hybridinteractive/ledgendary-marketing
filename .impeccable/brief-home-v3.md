# Home v3 (marketing, alternate)

Scope: third alternate of the Ledgendary marketing home, `site-v3/index.html`, shown beside v1 (`site/`) and v2 (`site-v2/`). Mode: Persuade. Static HTML, CSS, JS.
Audience, action, proof, claims: as PRODUCT.md (waitlist, agencies of one to hundreds, live UI with demo data, no invented claims, no prices).
Must differ from v1 (light, gold, autoplay loop, sticky story) and v2 (blue-drenched, lime, play-it-yourself) in look and signature; must match their product-animation richness and the voice winks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Scroll is time. The page is one evening-lit product canvas and the visitor's scroll moves a date scrubber from Oct 27 to Nov 18: the proposal goes out, gets viewed, chosen, signed, hours pile up day by day, the invoice is sent, waits, gets paid, posts. The books can be read as of any day, which is a real property of the product (reports reconstruct as of a date). Refuses the category default and both earlier alternates.

OWN-WORLD: Evening. Deep green-black ground #0e1613 with a raised panel tone #16211c, warm off-white #f3efe6 for text only, never as a ground; secondary text tinted from the green (#a9b8b0). One accent, signal coral #ff6b4a, used for the scrubber, the current-date marker, Paid and the primary button (ink text #0e1613 on coral). Product UI is drawn in its own dark theme, crisp 1px lines #24332c, no glow, no neon edges, no glass. Type: Bricolage Grotesque, display at large optical size and condensed width, weight 700 to 800, tight; text at small optical size. Tabular figures only in columns.

STORY: The visitor scrolls through twenty-three days of one job and watches every screen agree; then drags the scrubber themselves to read the books as of any date; meets roles at scale, privacy, the playbook; joins the waitlist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ground edge to edge. Nav. Headline set large and condensed at left ("Every day of the job, on one ledger." or stronger), subline, waitlist field plus coral button. Across the bottom third, the scrubber: a horizontal date rail Oct 27 → Nov 18 with event ticks (sent, viewed, chosen, signed, hours, invoiced, paid, posted) and a coral marker at Oct 27, with the product canvas rising behind it. A quiet "scroll, or drag the date" hint.

FORM: Category standard at full craft, third alternate; bar Linear, Stripe, Mercury, Apple product pages (scroll-scrubbed storytelling). Signature interactions: (1) scroll-scrubbed timeline: a pinned product canvas whose state is a pure function of the date; (2) "the books as of": a draggable date control in the books chapter that reconstructs receivables, the P&L and the journal for that day. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
