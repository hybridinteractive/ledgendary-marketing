# Look v4: Episodes (audience landing pages)

Scope: the v4 look's three audience pages, `site-v4/solo.html`, `site-v4/team.html`, `site-v4/enterprise.html`, plus a small `site-v4/index.html` that links them. Mode: Persuade. Content: `.impeccable/briefs/audiences.md`. Truth: `PRODUCT.md`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every feature is an episode. The page is structured like a season of a sitcom: each signature feature gets a title card ("S01E03 · The One Where the Invoice Pays Itself") followed by the product doing it, live. Fully in voice, and still a SaaS product page: the episode cards are typography and structure, never TV imagery, never naming any show. Refuses the category default and all three earlier looks.

OWN-WORLD: Full palette on white. Ground #ffffff; ink #121212; four saturated-but-soft section fields with named roles: mint #b8f0d4 (getting paid), sky #bfe2ff (time and people), peach #ffd0b5 (proposals and clients), lilac #dacbff (the books and trust). Each episode owns one field; product windows are white with ink 1.5px outlines and soft offset shadows. One hot accent for actions and Paid: tomato #ff4a1c as fill with white text only at large/bold sizes (check 3:1 large, use ink text otherwise). Type: Gabarito (400 to 900) for everything; display 800 to 900, tight; tabular figures in columns.

STORY: The visitor binge-watches: a "Previously on" recap strip in the hero shows the whole money path in five frames; then one episode per signature feature; a "Season finale" call to join the waitlist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White. Nav. Left: the audience headline huge, subline, waitlist form. Right or below: a horizontal "Previously on…" strip of five small live frames (proposal picked, hours logged, invoice sent, wait for it…, paid) that autoplay in sequence with a progress bar, pausable. The episode count for the page ("Season 1 · 7 episodes") as a quiet line.

FORM: Category standard at full craft, fourth look; bar Linear, Stripe, Mercury, plus the craft of a great streaming title sequence (type-led). Signature interactions: (1) episode title cards that set in with a type-led reveal as they enter, then the product demo plays beneath; (2) a sticky season index (episode list) at the side on desktop that tracks progress and jumps between episodes. seed: none (canon exit, new-work.md §3 "The standing exit"; user-pinned alternate, no roll).
